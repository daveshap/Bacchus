# Studio Ink

Use an off-white ground, dark ink, neutral secondary type, and restrained violet accents. Use distinct silhouettes, direct labels, and a consistent hierarchy. Keep unsupported decoration out of diagrams whose purpose is to explain a process.

Arrange titles, captions, and illustrations on a stable alignment. Use the accent for a selected item, an active state, or a data contrast. Move shared objects only when their change matters. Time reveals to the supporting words and let the completed state settle.

Use a quiet Ambient track with a small dynamic range when music is enabled. Keep the voice clear around visual reveals and technical vocabulary. Sound effects are off by default. Review any requested accent alongside the spoken phrase it marks.

Check that diagrams and titles remain readable through the busiest frame and that emphasis survives the transition to the next scene.
