# Warm Editorial

Use warm paper, deep green-black type, and rust highlights. Choose illustrations with distinct silhouettes and quiet detail. Group related elements with spacing and simple editorial rules. Give long labels and illustrations separate areas when they are both needed.

Open on a deliberate title and let each scene focus on a single part of the argument. Use editorial panels for contrasts and full illustrations for memorable subjects. Reveal a detail when it is named. Keep motion short and let important text settle between transitions.

Use a low Ambient bed when music is enabled. Leave room beneath narration and use cue-specific level changes where the argument becomes dense. Sound effects are off by default. Choose subtle accents only for events that benefit from them.

Review paper texture, baked edges, text readability, and the color of repeated subjects at their planned size. Confirm transitions support the argument and the approved soundtrack covers the cut.
